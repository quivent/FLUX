Multi-Critic Tribunal

h200-multi-critic · EGRL-tribunal

A second, differently-framed critic (InternVL) rides beside Pixtral. Both rank every candidate independently; where they disagree, that candidate is flagged for priority operator attention — exactly as EGRL treats an operator-vs-critic override: the highest-information event in the system.

Adaptive Reuse-Resident

h200-adaptive-coexist · EGRL-adaptive

The protocol that adapts to what is already on the card instead of demanding its own tenants. When this node runs other work — e.g. Surgery's two resident Gemma-31B servers on :18080 and :18081 — Beauty reuses them: one becomes the witness seat, the other the governor, and Beauty adds only the pieces surgery does not provide (FLUX, Pixtral, gates). It discovers resident vLLM servers via /v1/models and binds to whatever is live, preferring a real Qwen VLM for the witness if one appears.

The Ralph eye-gate

Gate 1 — builder observation

The witness reads the rendered frame with vision every iteration and scores it against explicit, numbered design laws. Unviewed output is undefined output; a candidate may only be submitted with its design-law checklist attached. Tracked metric: Blind Submission Rate = 0.

Gate 2 — independent critic

A critic that built nothing ranks all candidates against calibrated prior winners (the anchor set). Scores are comparative — "beats anchor X on axis Y" — never absolute vibes. Output is a ranked slate plus a typed evidence trace per candidate.

Gate 3 — operator eye-gate

The operator views the full slate and the verdict is final: it overrides the critic in both directions (a critic 9 can be killed; a 6.5 crowned). Every verdict is persisted verbatim to the taste log. A crown is the only path into the masterpiece vault.

Recalibration — the loop learns

After each generation: approved pieces enter the anchor set; every override is a measured critic-calibration error that retunes the critic prompt; provisional laws distilled from operator notes are tested and promoted; a favourable override of a low-scored candidate names a new approved register. The asset being built is the calibrated taste function, not any single frame.
