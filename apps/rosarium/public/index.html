About

Rosarium is a preserved exhibition of procedurally generated work. The pieces were made through FLUX and gathered here as a visual archive: a record of the studies, variations, and finished forms that grew from one sustained practice.

One thread runs through every collection: the bloom. A rose resolved as procedural geometry — petals, lattices, and tessellated scales — worn as couture, built as architecture, and always lit from within. Each engine takes the motif somewhere different, from the wireframe figures of the Crimson Ingress to the neon colonnades of the Vellum Study to the luminous gown of the Final Bloom.

The Gallery presents work as a flat grid. The Rotunda hangs the same collections as in a circular salon: one work held in light, with its neighbors waiting in the curve.
