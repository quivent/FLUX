The garden is waking

The practice

Made slowly,
without stopping.

A generative system proposes. A vision model looks back. The strongest images remain, and their lessons move through the next generation.

Tea is not a prompt box or an infinite scroll. It is an atelier in motion: a small set of visual laws, a memory of what has worked, and enough time for a point of view to emerge.

The ground beneath it

A San Francisco Compute Production

Tea lives on a San Francisco Compute node: serious GPU infrastructure that appears when the work calls, keeps the studio intact, and lets the machine disappear behind the making.
